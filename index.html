About

Hi, I’m John.

I’m originally from Texas and now live in rural Minnesota with my family. I’m a husband and a father. At home, conversations about programming practices, new technology, or games sometimes earn a patient smile and nod.

I’m a co-founder of Damned Cat Studio, bringing a software engineer’s perspective to independent game development.

I enjoy making complex things understandable, finding opportunities to automate, and helping teams grow. Meeting a client’s needs and making room for a healthy life both matter to me.

Professional work

Cloud architecture & engineering.

Security, compliance, cost, and operational needs are part of the architecture from the start. I aim to leave teams with systems they can own and maintain.

Elected service

Jackson City Council

City Council Member

Current

I serve as an elected member of the Jackson City Council. Alongside the regular work of the council, I bring my technical background to decisions about the city’s infrastructure and services.

I’ve worked with city staff to overhaul our IT footprint and strengthen security. The city processes utility payments and owns the ambulance service, so protecting those systems and meeting insurance requirements are practical responsibilities.

Animal welfare

Jackson County Animal Protection Society

President · Since its founding in 2020

Current

I was elected president of JCAPS when it was founded in 2020 and have served in that role since. We started the organization as residents responding to a gap in local animal control and rescue services.
